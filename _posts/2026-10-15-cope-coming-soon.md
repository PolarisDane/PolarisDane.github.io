A blog post on CoPE is on the way. In the meantime, check out the [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20597) and the [code](https://github.com/ulab-uiuc/CoPE).

CoPE adds multi-step action targets to agentic RL: on successful trajectories, the policy also learns to forecast the actions that follow each history, so acting and forecasting co-evolve within a single backbone, with no extra models and no inference overhead.

The upcoming post will walk through the motivation, the method, and results on ALFWorld, ScienceWorld, WebShop, τ²-bench, and AppWorld. Stay tuned!
